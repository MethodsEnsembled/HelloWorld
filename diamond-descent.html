Diamond Descent

Polish rough gems down to the perfect diamond.

Swipe (or press arrows) to slide gems diagonally.
Equal gems merge and halve: 1024 → 512 → … → 4 → 2.
A gem that reaches 2 becomes a perfect diamond and is collected.
Collect enough diamonds to beat the level, then descend deeper!
