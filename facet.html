FACET

A raw ruby starts at 2048. Every merge cuts the stone in half. Reach a perfect diamond — 2.

  1. Swipe diagonally (or tap the corner arrows). Stones slide along the crystal lattice.
  2. Two of the same value collide and become the next smaller cut.
  3. 2048 → 1024 → … → 4 → 2. A 2 is finished and will not cut again.
  4. Keep cutting after your first diamond. The vein dies when nothing can slide.
